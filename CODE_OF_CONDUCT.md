# Code of Conduct

This community is committed to a welcoming, safe, and equitable environment for
everyone who participates in good faith.

## Encouraged behavior

Community members are expected to:

- engage kindly, honestly, and constructively;
- respect different viewpoints, backgrounds, and levels of experience;
- accept and offer specific, actionable feedback;
- take responsibility for mistakes and help repair harm;
- respect confidentiality and the project's sensitive-data rules;
- credit sources and keep technical claims evidence-based.

## Restricted behavior

The following behavior is unacceptable:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or unwanted personal attention;
- insults, character attacks, discrimination, or sexualized conduct;
- disclosure of another person's private information;
- impersonation or attempts to evade moderation;
- commercial promotion unrelated to the community's purpose;
- publishing credentials, health information, account records, or other
  sensitive material.

## Scope

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report possible violations through the repository's
[private report form](https://github.com/antibagr/tbc-insurance-mcp/security/advisories/new)
and begin the title with `Community conduct:`. If the form is unavailable,
open a minimal issue requesting confidential contact and omit all incident
details.

The maintainer will review available evidence, protect confidentiality as far as
practical, and choose a proportionate response. Responses can include a private
warning, temporary participation limits, suspension, or a permanent ban.
Immediate restrictions may be used when safety requires them.
